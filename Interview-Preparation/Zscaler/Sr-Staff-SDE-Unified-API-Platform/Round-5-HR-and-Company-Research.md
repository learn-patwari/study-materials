# Round 5 — HR + Company Research

> **Format:** The HR/values round (sometimes with a senior leader). Assesses **culture fit,
> motivation, communication, and whether you've genuinely researched Zscaler**. Lower technical
> bar, but a weak "why Zscaler" or clashing with their stated values can sink an otherwise strong
> loop. Prepare this properly — it's the easiest round to ace with effort.

---

## Company research — know this cold

**What Zscaler is:** the company behind the **Zero Trust Exchange**, the world's largest
cloud-native security platform. It securely connects users, devices, and apps anywhere without
the traditional network perimeter — replacing "castle-and-moat" with **Zero Trust** ("never
trust, always verify"). Scale from the JD: **15M+ users, 185 countries, 150+ data centers.**

**Core products (one line each):**
- **ZIA** — secures outbound internet/SaaS traffic (secure web gateway, DLP, sandbox).
- **ZPA** — Zero Trust access to private apps; a VPN replacement.
- **ZDX** — monitors end-user digital experience across the whole path.

(Full depth in [`../README.md`](../README.md) and [`../Technical-Deep-Dive.md`](../Technical-Deep-Dive.md).)

**Positioning to mention:** AI-forward company leveraging "the world's largest security data
lake"; a leader in **SASE/SSE**; competes with Netskope, Palo Alto (Prisma), Cloudflare, and
Microsoft's built-in SSE. Founder/CEO **Jay Chaudhry**.

**How the Unified API Platform fits:** it's the connective tissue that makes all these products
programmable and consistent for customers and partners — standardized, governed APIs and a great
developer experience across the portfolio. Tie your pitch to *enabling the platform's ecosystem*.

## Common HR / behavioral questions & how to approach

- **"Why Zscaler?"** → Mission (securing the AI age / Zero Trust), scale (15M users, global),
  AI-forward culture, and the role being exactly your platform+AI strength. Be specific, not
  generic.
- **"Why this role / why leave your current job?"** → Growth into larger-scale, external
  developer platform + security domain; more impact. Never bad-mouth Samsung.
- **"Tell me about a conflict / disagreement."** → Challenge-culture answer: candid, respectful,
  data-driven; reached the best idea; preserved the relationship.
- **"A failure and what you learned."** → Real, owned, with the concrete change you made after.
- **"How do you handle ambiguity / competing priorities?"** → Break down, MVP, iterate,
  communicate; a real example.
- **"Where do you see yourself / why Staff+?"** → Deep technical impact + multiplying a team;
  aligns with "dynamic range" (strategy ↔ hands-on).
- **"Compensation expectations."** → Have a researched range for Sr Staff in Bengaluru (base +
  bonus + **RSUs/ESPP**); it's fine to say you're flexible for the right total package and want
  to understand the band.

**STAR reminder:** Situation → Task → Action (mostly "I", specific) → Result (quantified where
possible). Keep each to ~2 minutes.

# Zscaler — The Reverse Interview

> **What this is:** the questions *you* ask *them*. A "reverse interview" is where you evaluate
> the company, the team, and the role — and, done well, it also signals seniority and genuine
> interest. At 8+ years of experience, strong reverse-interview questions are expected; vague
> ones ("what's the culture like?") are a missed opportunity.

## How to use this

- **Don't ask all of these.** Pick 3–5 per interviewer, matched to *who they are* (hiring
  manager vs peer engineer vs skip-level vs recruiter).
- **Listen for specifics.** Good signs: concrete examples, honesty about challenges. Red flags:
  hand-wavy answers, everyone gives the same rehearsed line, defensiveness about turnover or
  on-call.
- **Take notes.** Reference their earlier answers later ("You mentioned X — how does that play
  into Y?"). It shows you're genuinely evaluating, not just performing.

---

## 1. About the Role & Success

- What does success look like in this role at **30 / 60 / 90 days**, and at one year?
- What are the **top 2–3 problems** you'd want this person to tackle first?
- Is this a **new role or a backfill**? (If backfill: what happened to the last person?)
- How is performance actually measured here — what separates a "meets" from an "exceeds"?
- What's the biggest **misconception** candidates have about this role?

## 2. About the Team & How It Works

- How is the team structured, and how does it interact with adjacent teams (product, SRE,
  security research)?
- Walk me through the **lifecycle of a change** — from idea to production. How long does it
  typically take?
- What does **on-call** look like — rotation frequency, pager load, and how healthy is it?
- How are technical decisions made and disagreements resolved? Can you give a recent example?
- What's the balance between **feature work, reliability/tech-debt, and firefighting** right now?

## 3. Engineering & Technical Depth (ask peers/hiring manager)

- Given Zscaler operates a **massive globally distributed proxy cloud**, what are the hardest
  **scale or latency** challenges the team is working on today?
- How do you handle **inline TLS inspection at scale** without becoming the performance
  bottleneck — where does the team spend its optimization effort?
- What does the **testing and deployment** story look like for something that sits in the
  critical path of customer traffic? How do you roll out safely?
- How much of the stack is **legacy vs modernized**, and where is the team investing?
- How do you balance **security/latency/cost** trade-offs when they conflict?

## 4. Product, Strategy & Business (ask hiring manager/skip-level)

- Zscaler is expanding beyond ZIA/ZPA into areas like **data protection, ZDX, and AI-driven
  security** — where is *this team* positioned in that roadmap?
- How does the team think about competition from **Netskope, Palo Alto (Prisma), Cloudflare,
  and Microsoft's built-in SSE**? What's the durable moat?
- Where is the biggest **growth bet** for the org over the next 1–2 years, and how does this
  role contribute?
- How is **AI** changing the product and the way the team builds — hype or real leverage here?

## 5. Culture, Growth & People

- How do you support **growth and promotion** — what's the path from this level to the next, and
  how long does it typically take?
- What's the **remote / hybrid** expectation for this team, realistically?
- How would you describe the **pace**? Zscaler has a reputation for moving fast — what does that
  mean day-to-day, and how does the team avoid burnout?
- What's kept **you** here, and what's one thing you'd genuinely change if you could?
- How diverse is the team, and how are differing viewpoints handled in technical debate?

## 6. For the Recruiter / Process

- What are the **remaining stages** and the timeline for a decision?
- Who are the interviewers and what will each **focus on**, so I can prepare well?
- Is there anything in my background you'd like me to **address or strengthen** before the next
  round?
- What **level/band** is this role mapped to, and what's the compensation structure (base /
  bonus / **RSUs / ESPP**)?

---

## Strong "signal" questions (pick one or two to stand out)

These show senior thinking:

- *"What's a recent decision the team got wrong, and what did you change afterward?"* — probes
  honesty and a learning culture.
- *"If I joined and in six months you were thrilled with the hire, what would I have done?"* —
  turns success criteria into something concrete and memorable.
- *"Where does this team create the most customer value, and where does it feel like the work
  isn't yet paying off?"* — shows you think in outcomes, not tasks.
- *"Zero Trust is now a crowded market — internally, what does Zscaler believe it does better
  than anyone else, and where are you still catching up?"* — shows you understand the landscape.

---

## Questions to AVOID (or handle carefully)

- ❌ Anything easily Googled ("What does Zscaler do?") — signals you didn't prepare.
- ❌ "What's the culture like?" as your *only* culture question — too generic; ask something
  specific instead.
- ⚠️ Comp/perks *too early* — fine with the recruiter, but don't lead with it in a technical
  round.
- ⚠️ "Do you monitor employees?" phrased as an accusation — you can probe work-life balance
  without sounding adversarial.

---

## Quick prep checklist

- [ ] Pick **3–5 questions per interviewer**, tailored to their role.
- [ ] Prepare **2 "signal" questions** for the hiring manager / skip-level.
- [ ] Have **process/timeline/comp** questions ready for the recruiter.
- [ ] Research each interviewer on LinkedIn and prepare **one personalized question** each.
- [ ] Jot answers down during the call and **follow up** on anything vague.

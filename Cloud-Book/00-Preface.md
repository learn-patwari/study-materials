# Preface

## Who this book is for

- **Complete beginners** who keep hearing "the cloud" and "AWS" and want to *actually*
  understand what people are talking about.
- **Students and career-switchers** who want a gentle, hands-on on-ramp before diving into
  certifications or deep technical docs.
- **Developers** from other backgrounds who can code but have never provisioned
  infrastructure.

You do **not** need to know networking, Linux, or any AWS service. You need curiosity and a
computer.

## How this book is organized

The book follows one deliberate arc:

> **Pain → Idea → Mechanics → Bricks → Examples → Do it yourself.**

1. First we feel the **pain** the cloud was invented to cure (Chapter 1). Nothing makes a
   solution memorable like understanding the problem.
2. Then we introduce **the idea** of cloud computing (Chapter 2) and **how it works**
   (Chapter 3).
3. We tour the **building blocks** (Chapter 4) and see them combined in **real examples**
   (Chapter 5).
4. Finally, you **do it yourself** — create a free account safely (Chapter 6) and build real
   things in the **hands-on labs** (Chapter 7).
5. A **glossary** and **next steps** (Chapter 8) send you off with a map for going deeper.

## Conventions used in this book

| You'll see | It means |
|------------|----------|
| 💡 **Key idea** | A concept worth remembering |
| ⚠️ **Watch out** | A common mistake or a way to accidentally get charged |
| 🧪 **Try it** | A hands-on action you can take |
| `code font` | Something you type into a terminal or a value you enter |
| **Key Takeaways** | A quick recap at the end of each chapter |

Commands that start with `$` are typed into your computer's terminal (don't type the `$`
itself). Console steps ("click **Create bucket**") refer to the AWS website.

## A note on cost

This book is built around the **AWS Free Tier**. Every hands-on step is designed to stay
within free limits, and **every lab includes a cleanup step**. In Chapter 6 you'll set up a
**budget alarm** so you're notified before spending a single cent. Cloud bills only surprise
people who skip these safety steps — you won't.
